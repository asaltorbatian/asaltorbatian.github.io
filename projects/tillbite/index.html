Product overview

One connected platform for restaurant operations

TillBite brings front-of-house ordering, customer records, phone and online orders, kitchen printing and payment processing into one database-driven commercial platform.

The product is currently deployed in a working restaurant and is being prepared for wider commercial release. Its dedicated mobile application also lets customers browse the menu and place orders from their smartphones.
