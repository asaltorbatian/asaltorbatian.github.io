Project overview

One connected system for restaurant operations

TillBite brings core restaurant workflows into a database-driven business system. It is designed to support front-of-house ordering, customer records, phone and online orders, kitchen printing and payment processing.

The project also includes a mobile application that lets customers browse the menu and place orders from their smartphones.
