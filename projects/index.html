TillBite Restaurant POS System

A complete commercial restaurant platform currently deployed in a working restaurant.

TillBite is a full-stack commercial restaurant platform built with a database-driven architecture. It includes a powerful POS system, customer management, phone and online ordering, kitchen printing, payment processing and many other business features.

The product is currently deployed and operating in a working restaurant as it prepares for wider commercial release. It also provides a dedicated mobile application, allowing customers to browse the menu and place online orders quickly and seamlessly from their smartphones.
